ASSUMING DIRECT CONTROL.

Ever wanted to actually play the video game?
Swing a close match yourself?
Completely whiff an ult and cost your team the championship?
Grief your teammates personally instead of merely watching them grief themselves?

NOW YOU CAN!!!

Harbinger Direct Control turns Teamfight Manager 2's normally pre-simulated watched match into a real-time simulation you can interfere with. Take over ANY of the ten champions on EITHER team. Issue actual movement and attack commands, aim abilities, swap champions, or run it down mid and feed under tower with your own two hands.

Pick/ban works exactly as before. Shopping stays automatic. The base game still handles pathfinding, champion logic, combat and the simulation. You just get to make everyone else's problems considerably more personal.

THE BUTTONS
Wait for the ready prompt, then Ctrl+Home starts the live match. F1-F10 selects a champion from the visible player cards. RMB moves or attacks (yes, you can hold it); A + LMB attack-moves; Q/W/R uses skills; H holds; B returns. MMB drags the camera, and the wheel zooms. End hands your champion back to AI without stopping the live match. Confirm Ctrl+End to surrender Direct Control permanently for that match.

IMPORTANT: The game's DEFAULT shortcuts already occupy some of the keys I borrowed. Open Settings > Shortcuts and rebind any conflicting native actions, especially the ones overlapping your Direct Control skills and orders. I'll absolutely implement proper customizable mod keybinds later. I promise. Probably.

KNOWN ISSUES / FUNCTIONALITY GAPS
- A bit of pre-simulation is still required before the battlefield becomes interactive. Wait for the synchronization/ready prompt; Start Match isn't broken or secretly running a forty-minute loading screen.
- Some champion mechanics still need special handling. Gunfighter, for example, doesn't preserve native move-while-attacking with attack-move.
- Some champions have skill descriptions that don't match their actual in-game targeting or behavior. Direct Control follows the game's actual runtime skill rules, not the prose. Weird interactions are worth reporting.
- AI-responsive player pings and manual shopping are not implemented. Shopping still happens automatically.
- I still haven't figured out how to get the AI to flame me yet.
- Direct Control keeps replay-seek shortcuts disabled while it owns the live match. Ctrl+End restores normal replay; temporary End does not.
- Single-player only for this release. Windows/Steam, targeting the verified TFM2 v0.6.1 executable. Game updates may require another mod update. Deliberately fixed 1x live simulation.

OTHER MODS
Mods that change champion AI/decision-making, movement/targeting/combat, automatic ability execution, native inputs, or simulation timing/replay are the most likely to collide with Harbinger. Mods that just add champions, items, teams or stats are less likely to interfere, but combinations aren't broadly tested. If something acts haunted, try Harbinger by itself before reporting a bug.
