[h2]IMPORTANT NOTES:[/h2]
[h3]MATCH START PRE-SIMULATION[/h3]
The game requires some pre-simulation time before the battlefield becomes interactive, so initial loading and synchronization may take a little longer than usual. Wait for the ready prompt before pressing Ctrl+Home at the start of a game.

[h3]SHORTCUT SETTINGS[/h3]
Some default spectator shortcuts conflict with the controls I've chosen. Harbinger's F1-F10 champion selection works independently of the game's native follow bindings, even with duplicate player names or a hidden UI. F1-F5 always select your team and F6-F10 select the opposing team, regardless of which side is blue or red. Feel free to rebind the game's native follow shortcuts, but they may still move the camera if assigned to the same keys.

Please check your Shortcut settings, especially 'R' (Auto Follow). 'Q' and 'A' also overlap replay actions, but Harbinger suppresses those replay actions while it owns the live match. Selecting a champion doesn't independently center or follow the camera; MMB drag and scroll-wheel zoom are the supported camera controls. I'll implement customizable mod keybindings and dedicated camera-follow controls later. I promise!
