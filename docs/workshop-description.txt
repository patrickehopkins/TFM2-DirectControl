[h1]Harbinger Direct Control[/h1]

[h2]ASSUMING DIRECT CONTROL.[/h2]

Ever wanted to actually play the video game?

Swing a close match yourself?

Completely whiff an ult and cost your team the championship?

Grief your teammates personally instead of merely watching them grief themselves?

[b]NOW YOU CAN!!![/b]

Harbinger Direct Control turns Teamfight Manager 2's pre-simulated matches into a real-time simulation you can interfere with.

Take control of [b]ANY champion on EITHER team[/b]. Move, attack, cast abilities, swap champions, or run it down mid and feed under tower.

Pick/ban remains unchanged. The game's underlying combat, pathfinding, and automatic shopping still work normally. You're simply introducing another opportunity for catastrophic decision-making.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]WHAT IT DOES[/h2]
[list]
[*]Directly control any of the ten champions.
[*]Manual movement, attacking, attack-move, and abilities.
[*]Camera dragging, zooming, and automatic team fog.
[*]Temporary AI handoff and permanent release.
[*]Replay protection to keep your commands synchronized with the visible match.
[/list]

[h2]CONTROLS[/h2]
[table]
[tr][th]Input[/th][th]Action[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Ctrl+Home[/td][td]Begin Direct Control[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]F1-F10[/td][td]Select champion[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]RMB[/td][td]Move / Attack[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]A + LMB[/td][td]Attack-move[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Q / W / R[/td][td]Abilities[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]H / B[/td][td]Hold / Return[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]MMB / Wheel[/td][td]Camera drag / Zoom[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]End[/td][td]Return champion to AI[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Ctrl+End[/td][td]Permanently release control (confirmation required)[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[b]Wait for the initial synchronization and ready prompt before pressing Ctrl+Home.[/b]

[b]IMPORTANT:[/b] Some default spectator shortcuts conflict with these controls. Check R and F1-F10 in the game's Shortcuts menu. Q and A also overlap replay actions, but Harbinger suppresses those replay actions while it owns the live match. I'll implement customizable mod keybindings later. I promise!

[hr][/hr]
[h2]KNOWN ISSUES / FUNCTIONALITY GAPS[/h2]
[list]
[*]Some champion mechanics still need special handling. For example, Gunfighter doesn't preserve his native move-while-attacking behavior during attack-move.
[*]Certain champions have skill descriptions that don't accurately represent their actual in-game functionality. Harbinger follows the game's underlying skill mechanics.
[*]The game requires some pre-simulation before the battlefield becomes interactive. Wait for the ready prompt before taking control.
[*]Player-issued AI pings and manual shopping aren't implemented. Ordinary automatic shopping remains functional.
[*]I still haven't figured out how to make the AI flame me yet.
[/list]

[h2]COMPATIBILITY[/h2]
[b]Windows/Steam, Teamfight Manager 2 v0.6.1. Single-player only.[/b]

Mods that modify champion AI, combat, targeting, native inputs, or simulation timing could conflict with Harbinger. Content-focused mods may be compatible, but combinations haven't been comprehensively tested.

Multiplayer support isn't implemented.
